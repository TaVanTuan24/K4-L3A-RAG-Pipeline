## Study at VU

VU's presence is global, and includes well-established teaching partnerships across Asia. When you study with us, you will be part of a truly international institution.

Study at a leading Australian university that offers:

- 100-plus years of experience in the education sector

- pathways from vocational education to postgraduate qualifications.

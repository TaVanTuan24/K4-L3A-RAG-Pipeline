Victoria University has seven libraries across its campuses in Melbourne, and provides library services within the learning hubs at VU Sydney and VU Brisbane. Campus libraries have a variety of amenities and services including access to computers, wireless connectivity and printing facilities.

During library opening hours, books can be returned to any campus library. Books can also be returned to campus libraries with an external return chute (St Albans and Werribee) any time. Facilities, services and opening hours may differ between libraries, and all services finish 15 minutes before the closing time.

We understand that the rising cost of living is making things more challenging for students. We're committed to supporting you with services and resources, so you can focus on your studies.

If cost-of-living pressures are impacting your studies or day-to-day life, we encourage you to reach out to our VUHQ team.

Find information about support services and resources available to you.

## Financial support

Get an interest-free loan to help you purchase trade tools and supplies while being an apprentice.

You can apply for an AASL if you are completing an Australian Apprenticeship in a qualification listed on the Australian Apprenticeship Priority List.

Find out more about Australian Apprenticeship Support Loans

VU hosts a free Tax Help Service from July until the end of October, in which a volunteer trained by the Australian Tax Office (ATO) provides one-to-one assistance and support to help students complete their tax return online.

If you earn $70K or less a year and have simple tax affairs, you may be eligible for free assistance through the Tax Help program at Victoria University. Find out more and check your eligibility.

To book a free appointment at Footscray Park campus with our Tax Help volunteer:

- Call +61 3 9919 5400

- Book online (select Appointment Location: Footscray Park, Service Type: Tax Help Service).

The Australian Government provides payments to students who are studying, such as Youth Allowance, Austudy, Abstudy etc. All of these payments have eligibility criteria and will require you to apply directly to Services Australia – Centrelink.

Our Student Welfare team can provide guidance on which Centrelink payments you may be eligible for. You can also use Centrelink's Payment and Service Finder to help find what payments you may be eligible for based on your circumstances.

If you are enrolled in a Certificate IV or below TAFE course, we recommend you apply for a Low Income Health Care Card . This card can give you access to reduced TAFE tuition fees. Even if you are not eligible for other student payments, you may still be eligible for a Low-Income Health Care Card.

Learn more about Centrelink support services for students

Our Student Welfare team can offer assistance, referrals and advice to help you manage your finances while studying. This includes:

- advice about managing your study and living related costs

- advice about utility bills, providers, and Government relief grant schemes

- advice about credit card debts, personal debts, mobile phone plans and fines

- information about Centrelink payments .

We cannot provide funds to help you pay your course fees.

If you're on a low income, you may be eligible for a short-term, interest-free loan through Good Shepherd. These loans can help you pay for essential items.

Learn more about the No Interest Loan Scheme

We offer scholarships to help students with their living and education costs.

Browse our scholarships

Access a Victorian Government utility relief grant to get help with paying overdue energy or water bills. These grants are available for low-income Victorians experiencing unexpected hardship.

Learn more about the grants

## Academic & personal support

The Victoria University Mental Health Support Line operates around the clock. You can access immediate and short-term support 24 hours a day, including weekends and public holidays.

Contact us to get connected with a support worker:

- Call 1300 511 971

- Text 0485 908 389

Access short-term confidential counselling for stress, anxiety, study pressures or personal concerns.

Learn more about our counselling services

The Learning Hub offers personalised support, workshops and online resources to help you develop the skills you need to succeed at any stage of your studies.

Visit the Learning Hub to get help with writing, maths, referencing and study skills.

Our trained student mentors can help support you as you settle into university.

They can guide you in using VU systems, like MyVU and VU Collaborate, and help you navigate administrative processes such as applying for special consideration or advanced standing.
